Employees may be reimbursed for approved business expenses submitted within 30 days.
Meal reimbursements are capped at $40 per day while traveling for business.
All expenses over $75 require an itemized receipt, not just a card statement.
Reimbursement requests are processed through the finance portal and paid within 10 business days.
Personal expenses, alcohol, and entertainment are not reimbursable unless pre-approved.
