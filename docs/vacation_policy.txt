All full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid vacation per calendar year.
Vacation days accrue at a rate of 1.25 days per month.
Unused vacation days may roll over up to a maximum of 5 days into the next year.
Employees must submit vacation requests at least 2 weeks in advance through the HR portal.
Part-time employees accrue vacation on a pro-rated basis depending on hours worked.
